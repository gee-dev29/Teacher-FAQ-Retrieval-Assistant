Attendance Policy

Teachers must take attendance at the start of every class and submit it to the school office by the end of the day. A student is marked late if they arrive more than ten minutes after the class starts.

A student who misses more than three classes in a row without a note from a parent or guardian must be reported to the school counselor. The teacher should also call the parent to find out why the student has been away.

Students who are absent for a school event, such as a sports competition, are marked as excused and are not counted against their attendance record.
