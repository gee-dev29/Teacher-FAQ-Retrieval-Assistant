Staff Leave Policy

A teacher who wants to take sick leave must inform the head teacher before the school day starts, either by phone or message. Sick leave of more than two days needs a doctor's note.

Teachers get ten days of paid personal leave each school year. A leave request must be submitted at least one week in advance, except in an emergency.

Maternity and paternity leave follow the national labor law and must be applied for through the school's human resources office, not the head teacher directly.
