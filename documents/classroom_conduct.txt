Classroom Conduct Policy

A student who disrupts a class repeatedly gets a verbal warning first, then a written warning if the behavior continues. A third incident in the same term means the student is sent to the head teacher's office.

Teachers are not allowed to send a student out of the classroom without supervision. If a student must leave the room, another staff member or the office must be informed first.

Phones are not allowed to be used during class time unless the teacher gives permission for a specific lesson activity. A phone used without permission can be kept by the teacher until the end of the day.
