Grading Policy

Teachers must enter grades into the school system within five working days after a test or assignment is marked. Late entry needs written approval from the head of department.

Final grades are made up of 40 percent classwork, 20 percent homework, and 40 percent exams. A student who misses an exam for a valid reason, such as illness with a doctor's note, gets a makeup exam instead of a zero.

If a parent disputes a grade, the teacher must show the marked script to the parent within three school days of the request. Grades can only be changed by the teacher who set the test, not by any other staff member.
