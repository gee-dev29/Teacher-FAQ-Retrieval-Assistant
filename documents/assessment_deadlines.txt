Assessment Deadlines Policy

Assignments handed in late without a reason lose five percent of the mark for each day they are late, up to a maximum of five days. After five days late, the assignment is marked zero.

A student can ask for a deadline extension before the due date. The teacher can approve up to three extra days without needing permission from the head of department.

Group projects must have all deadlines agreed on at the start of the project and written down, so that every student in the group knows the same dates.
