Staff Grievance Procedure

A teacher with a complaint about another staff member should first try to settle it directly with that person. If that does not work, the teacher should write down the complaint and give it to the head teacher.

The head teacher must respond to a written grievance within five working days. If the teacher is not happy with the response, they can take the complaint to the school board.

Grievances about pay or contracts are handled by human resources, not the head teacher, because these need to follow labor law rules.
